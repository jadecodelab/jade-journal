# Project: Jade journal and reflection

## Vision

Build a modern AI-powered personal journal application that is designed for long-term personal growth instead of simply storing diary entries.

This application is for ONE user (me).

The goal is to make journaling effortless while helping me reflect on my life over months and years.

Privacy is the highest priority.

The app should feel calm, minimal, beautiful, and distraction-free.

Think Apple Notes meets Day One meets ChatGPT, but focused on personal growth.

---

# Core Principles

1. My journal belongs to me.
2. AI should assist, never replace my writing.
3. The journal should help me remember things I forgot.
4. The journal should help me notice my growth.
5. Everything should be simple and elegant.
